About the creators

Made by the creators of The Awesome Marketers

Awesome Meets is one-to-one networking in Helsinki: it introduces you to someone new from your field for a real conversation. It comes from the people who built The Awesome Marketers community.

Why Awesome Meets exists

For years we've preached that networking is everything. After talking to so many of you, I wanna make it actually happen: in person, over food, with people you'd never bump into otherwise.

Anna

So that's what this is. You fill in a short form, I match you with 1 or 2 people, and you meet over lunch, an after-work coffee or a walk.

It started in October 2026 as Awesome Autumn Meets, a round for marketers from the community: 40 people signed up. Awesome Meets itself is for everyone, whatever you do, and you don't need to be a member of The Awesome Marketers.

The Awesome Marketers

The Awesome Marketers is an English-speaking marketing community based in Finland, founded by Anna Pogrebniak in Helsinki in 2019. As of 2026, the community has grown to more than 1,800 members. Most are marketers living and working in Finland, and many originally moved here from other countries.

The community runs on Slack and is organised entirely by volunteers. Members share job openings, ask for advice, and help each other with things like salary negotiation, freelancing, and finding their way in the Finnish job market. The community also runs events in Helsinki: afterworks, webinars and meetups.

Who runs Awesome Meets

Me, Anna Pogrebniak. I read every sign-up and put the matches together myself, with help from Claude, an AI assistant. When you email Awesome Meets, you're emailing me.

A little about me: I'm a fractional growth marketer. I've spent 11 years in B2B SaaS marketing, and these days I join software companies part-time to build and run their marketing. In 2019 I founded The Awesome Marketers in Helsinki, and Awesome Meets is the newest thing to grow out of it. My own work lives at awesomemarketer.fi.

Awesome Meets is also on LinkedIn.

Questions people ask

What is Awesome Meets?

Awesome Meets is one-to-one networking in Helsinki. You tell us about your interests, your industry and your profession, we introduce you to someone new from your field, and the two of you meet in person over lunch, coffee or a walk.

How does Awesome Meets work?

There are four steps. You fill in a short form about yourself. Every person in the community is reviewed before they are matched. We match you with someone you have something in common with, never at random. Then the two of you meet in person.

Who is Awesome Meets for?

Awesome Meets is for anyone working in Finland who wants to meet someone new who isn't a colleague. It started with marketers from The Awesome Marketers community, but you don't need to be a marketer.

Where does Awesome Meets run?

Awesome Meets started in Helsinki, and for now I can only guarantee a match in the Helsinki region. You can sign up from anywhere in Finland: I keep your sign-up until I find you a proper match nearby, and the form asks whether you could travel to Helsinki or Tampere. If you live outside Finland, tell me where you are on the homepage, and you'll be the first to know if Awesome Meets comes to your city.

When is the next round of Awesome Meets?

Round one took place in October 2026 with 40 people, and round two in November 2026 with 42. You can sign up at any time. Each round has a cut-off date: sign up before it and you are matched in that round, sign up after it and you are in the next one. The current cut-off is on the sign-up page.

Do I need to be a member of The Awesome Marketers?

No. Awesome Meets grew out of that community, but it is open to everyone, and you don't need to be a marketer either.

How do you choose my match?

By hand. I read every sign-up and match by what you do and who you'd like to meet: your job, your industry, where you are in your career, where you are and the wishes you tick in the form. Claude, an AI assistant, helps by suggesting who could fit, and I decide every match myself. No match is random.

What if I change my mind or can't make it?

Before the round's cut-off, the confirmation email has a button that takes you off the list, for that round only or for all rounds. After that, reply to my email. And if you've already been matched and plans change, that's fine: just tell your match.

Who runs Awesome Meets?

Awesome Meets is run by Anna Pogrebniak, founder of The Awesome Marketers, an English-speaking marketing community founded in Helsinki in 2019.
