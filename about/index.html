About the creators

Made by the creators of The Awesome Marketers

Awesome Meets is a matching format for marketers in Helsinki. It comes from the people who built The Awesome Marketers community.

Why Awesome Meets exists

For years we've preached that networking is everything. After talking to so many of you, I wanna make it actually happen: in person, over food, with marketers you'd never bump into otherwise.

Anna

So that's what this is. You fill in a short form, I match you with 1 or 2 marketers, and you meet over lunch, an after-work coffee or a walk.

Round one was Awesome Autumn Meets: 40 marketers signed up for October 2026.

The Awesome Marketers

The Awesome Marketers is an English-speaking marketing community based in Finland, founded by Anna Pogrebniak in Helsinki in 2019. As of 2026, the community has grown to over 1,823 members. Most are marketers living and working in Finland, and many originally moved here from other countries.

The community runs on Slack and is organised entirely by volunteers. Members share job openings, ask for advice, and help each other with things like salary negotiation, freelancing, and finding their way in the Finnish job market. The community also runs events in Helsinki: afterworks, webinars and meetups.

Who runs Awesome Meets

Me, Anna Pogrebniak. I read every sign-up and put the matches together myself, with help from Claude, an AI assistant. When you email Awesome Meets, you're emailing me.

Awesome Meets is also on LinkedIn.

Questions, ideas, or something that didn't work? Write to anna@awesomemarketers.fi.
