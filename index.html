How it works

From a short form to a real conversation

Sign-ups close on . By everyone gets their match, and you meet between .

  1. You fill in the form

    It takes a few minutes: who you are, what you could talk about, and where and when you could meet.

  2. I put the matches together

    I read every answer and match you with 1 or 2 marketers. Claude, an AI assistant, helps me find good matches.

  3. You get your match

    A message on LinkedIn and an email, with the place, the date and the time.

  4. You meet

    In person, in Helsinki. From there it's up to you and your match.

Questions you might have

Who is it for?

Marketers in and around Helsinki. In-house, agency, freelance, students and interns, and marketers between jobs are all welcome.

How many people will I meet?

You choose: 1 or 2 marketers per round.

Who sees my answers?

Only me, Anna. I use Claude, an AI assistant, to help me find good matches. 🔒 topics are never shared with your match, unless you both picked the same one. The details are on Your data.

What if I change my mind?

Just reply to any email from me and I'll take you off the list.

What if I can't make it on the day?

Reply to your match email and I'll let your match know.
