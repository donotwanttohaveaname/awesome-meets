How does it work?

  1. Today

    You fill in the form. What you do, where you are and how you’d like to meet. It takes a couple of minutes.

  2. The cut-off

    The round’s cut-off. I check every sign-up, then match everyone who’s in. There’s a new round every month.

  3. A few days later

    Your match lands in your inbox. One person, or two if you chose a trio. No random matches: I match by what you do and who you’d like to meet.

  4. After that

    You meet. You pick the day and the place together: lunch, an after-work coffee or a walk.

Why bother?

We all know people at work.

Meeting someone new is rarer. Meetups and conferences give you plenty of names and LinkedIn connections. A good conversation is harder to come by.

It might never lead to a job.

It might lead to something much better: a client, a collaborator, a friend, or a much more interesting Tuesday lunch.

It’s the part of work life you can’t turn into KPIs. Unless your KPI is happiness.
