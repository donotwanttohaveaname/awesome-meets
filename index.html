When did you last meet someone from your field who isn't a colleague?

The job market is really hard right now.

Your connections matter more than ever.

And yet so many of us have no time or energy left for networking.

Networking isn't saying “Hello, my name is” at a meetup or a conference.

Networking is making real connections. Learning about the other person, their work and what they've achieved.

Networking won't bring you a job tomorrow.

But a person you meet today might introduce you to a co-founder, an employer or a client years from now.

It's the part of work life you can't turn into KPIs.

Unless your KPI is happiness, thanks to a rich social life.

So, again: when was the last time you truly networked?
