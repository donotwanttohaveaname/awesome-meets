We all know people at work. But how often do you meet someone genuinely new? Meetups and conferences give you plenty of names and LinkedIn connections. A good conversation is harder to come by.

Networking isn't saying “Hello, my name is” at a meetup or a conference. It's a proper conversation with someone you'd probably never have met otherwise.

It might never lead to a job. It might lead to something much better. Some people become clients. Some become collaborators. Some become friends. Some just make your Tuesday lunch much more interesting.

It's the part of work life you can't turn into KPIs. Unless your KPI is happiness.

So, again: when was the last time you truly networked?
