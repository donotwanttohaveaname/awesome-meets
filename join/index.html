Your data in Awesome Meets

  • Who has access: only me, Anna. No one else can see your answers. I use Claude, an AI assistant, to help me find good matches.
  • What it's used for: matching you with one person, or two for a trio. Nothing else.
  • What your match sees: your name, job title, company, LinkedIn and email address, so you can get in touch.
  • How you'll hear from me: by email, from anna@meet.awesomemarketers.fi, sent through Resend, an email delivery service. Replies land in my normal inbox.
  • After the meetup: I send you a short feedback questionnaire. Answering is up to you.
  • Where it's stored: a private Google Sheet in The Awesome Marketers' Google Workspace.
  • How long: deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.
