Your data

What I collect, and what I do with it

The short version: only I can see your answers, I use them to match you and for nothing else, and you can have them deleted whenever you like.

When you sign up for a round

The sign-up form asks for your name, your email, your LinkedIn profile if you want to share it, a few things about your work, the topics you'd like to talk about, and where and when you could meet.

  • Who has access: only me, Anna. No one else can see your answers. I use Claude, an AI assistant, to help me find good matches.
  • What it's used for: matching you with 1 or 2 marketers. Nothing else.
  • How you'll hear from me: when you're matched, I send you a message on LinkedIn and by email with the place, date and time. From there it's up to you and your match to continue. Emails come from anna@meet.awesomemarketers.fi and are sent through Resend, an email delivery service. Replies land in my normal inbox.
  • What your match sees: your name, your job title and company if you gave them, your LinkedIn profile if you gave it, and the topics you both picked.
  • After the meetup: I send you a short follow-up questionnaire asking for your feedback.
  • 🔒 topics: never shared with your match, unless you both picked the same one.
  • Where it's stored: a private Google Sheet in The Awesome Marketers' Google Workspace.
  • How long: deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

When you join the waitlist

  • What I keep: only your email and the time you left it.
  • What it's used for: one thing, sending you the invitation when the next round opens.
  • Where it's stored: the same private Google Sheet, in its own tab.
  • How to get off the list: ask me any time and I'll take your email off.

This website

  • No cookies and no tracking. There are no analytics, ad pixels or social media trackers on this site.
  • One small thing saved in your browser: the number of marketers who signed up, so the counter shows up straight away on your next visit. It says nothing about you and never leaves your browser.
  • Hosting and fonts: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website, loading it means your browser sends your IP address to those two services.

See it, change it, delete it

Want to know what I have on you, fix something, or have it all deleted? Email me at anna@awesomemarketers.fi and I'll sort it out. You can also just reply to any email you got from me.
